# User Summary - Quick Start

## 🚀 3 Steps to Personalize Your AI

### Step 1: Open Settings
Click the gear icon ⚙️ in the chat window.

### Step 2: Add Your Summary
In the "Your Summary" section (at the top), type your resume/profile:

```
I'm Alex Johnson, a Senior iOS Developer with 5 years of experience.
I work at Google on the Maps team.
Skills: Swift, SwiftUI, Python, React
Education: BS Computer Science, Stanford
```

### Step 3: Save
Click "Save" button at the top-right.

---

## ✅ Test It

Ask the AI:
```
"What's my experience?"
"Where do I work?"
"What are my skills?"
```

The AI will answer using your summary!

---

## 💡 What to Include

- **Name & Role**: Who you are, what you do
- **Experience**: Years of experience, companies
- **Skills**: Programming languages, frameworks, tools
- **Education**: Degree, university
- **Projects**: Current and past projects
- **Interests**: What you're learning, career goals

---

## 📊 Example

### Your Summary:
```
Name: Sarah Chen
Role: Full-Stack Developer at Microsoft
Experience: 3 years
Skills: JavaScript, TypeScript, React, Node.js
Education: MS Computer Science, MIT
Currently: Building cloud-native applications
```

### AI Response:
```
You: "What do I do?"
AI: "You're a Full-Stack Developer at Microsoft with 3 years 
     of experience, specializing in JavaScript, TypeScript, 
     React, and Node.js."
```
